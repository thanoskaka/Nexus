# Sample Portfolio Mode

Nexus Portfolio includes a sample/demo portfolio mode that lets new users explore the application before adding real financial data.

## How it works

1. When you first sign in and have no assets, click **"Explore sample portfolio"** on the Dashboard.
2. A pre-built sample portfolio loads with cash, stocks/ETFs, mutual funds, gold/commodity, and cross-market holdings (Canada, India, U.S.).
3. A **"Sample data"** badge appears across Dashboard, Assets, and Settings.
4. You can freely explore charts, tables, and settings with sample data.

## Clearing sample data

- Go to **Settings → Data** tab and click **"Clear Sample Data"**.

## Limitations

- Sample data is stored locally in your browser only. It does not sync to Firebase or any cloud backend.
- Adding real assets or importing holdings while sample mode is active automatically clears the sample portfolio.
- Exporting sample data includes a warning that the data is not real.
